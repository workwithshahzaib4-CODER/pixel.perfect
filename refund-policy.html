Customer care

Refund policy

Approved refunds are issued when an order cannot be fulfilled or when Pixel Perfect confirms a damaged-order resolution requires a refund.

How refunds work

Contact support with your order ID, phone number, and issue details. After approval, refunds are sent to the original payment method where possible. Processing time depends on the payment provider.

COD orders

COD orders that have not shipped can be cancelled through support. Orders already delivered follow the return policy.
